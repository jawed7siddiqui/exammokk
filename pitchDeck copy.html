Market Problem


The Problem

Why blind test-taking fails 50M+ competitive exam aspirants
Structural Gap
“Aspirants solve dozens of mock tests, but their scores plateau because legacy platforms only show a final scorecard—never diagnosing why they lost marks or what to study next.”
01
BLIND MOCK TEST LOOPS

Existing platforms tell students: “You scored 45/100.” They offer static PDF keys without identifying micro-topic knowledge leaks or why mistakes happened repeatedly.

02
TIME-ACCURACY TRAPS & NEGATIVE MARKS

Competitive exams are lost on negative marking. Students spend 120+ seconds on trap questions and mark them wrong, lacking analytics to build disciplined skipping strategy.

03
EXPENSIVE & BLOATED PLATFORMS

Legacy test series are bundled into costly course packages (₹2,000–₹5,000/yr) filled with heavy video players that buffer and lag on budget smartphones across Tier 2/3 towns.

04
LACK OF NEXT-STEP GUIDANCE

After taking a 2-hour mock, a student is left alone with a massive 100-page solution sheet. There is zero personalized guidance prescribing their immediate study priorities.

The Market Reality
Students don't need more question banks; they need diagnostic intelligence that detects concept leaks and engineers measurable score improvements.

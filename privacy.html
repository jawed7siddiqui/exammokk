KidsPaint AI

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Overview

KidsPaint AI ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to providing a safe, creative, and educational environment for students, educators, and families. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when using our application and website.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Credentials: Mobile phone number (provided by adult users, parents, or guardians) used strictly for authentication and account security.
  • Profile Details: Display name/first name, gender selection, and learning level/grade to customize AI drawing tools and creative suggestions.
  • Artwork & Creations: Canvas creations saved locally or on secured servers for user retrieval.

3. Child Safety & COPPA Compliance

We comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policies:

  • We do not collect unnecessary Personally Identifiable Information (PII) from children.
  • Account registration and sign-in require parental or educator supervision.
  • We never sell or trade any user information to third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

All communication between our application and servers is encrypted using Secure Sockets Layer (SSL / HTTPS). Local progress is cached securely in your device browser storage (`localStorage`).

5. Third-Party Services

We do not integrate behavioural advertising SDKs or unauthorized trackers. Any third-party tools utilized are strictly for basic infrastructure, performance monitoring, and app uptime.

6. Data Deletion & Contact Us

Parents and users have the right to request deletion of their account profile and saved data at any time. For support or privacy questions, contact our team:

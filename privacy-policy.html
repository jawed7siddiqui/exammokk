Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Overview

ExamMokk Cloud ("we", "our", or "us") provides a standardized online examination simulation workspace. We are committed to maintaining secure, transparent data safeguards for candidates practicing mock tests, sectional time drills, and daily general knowledge challenges across India.

2. Information Collection & Scope

To run our standardized evaluation simulators efficiently, the platform interfaces with the following scope of non-sensitive parameters:

  • Authentication Logs: Mobile number or basic identity references parsed during dashboard access to link mock score histories safely.
  • Response & Evaluation Inputs: Individual test selections, timestamp markings per question, and test engine attempts required to compute All-India percentile calculations.
  • Local Cache Metrics: Ongoing sectional answer states stored transiently within browser localStorage to prevent state loss during potential connectivity resets.

3. Data Infrastructure & Security

All transactional endpoints, analytical computations, and API score updates are fully safeguarded via high-grade Secure Sockets Layer (SSL / HTTPS) transit encryption protocols. We enforce a strict non-disclosure regime and do not expose or monetize individual testing analytics or personal identifiers to secondary marketing networks.

4. Third-Party Interfaces & Tracking

ExamMokk implements only fundamental infrastructure controllers, cloud performance measurement metrics, and base metrics needed to monitor system up-time. We explicitly bar any behavioural tracking SDKs or unauthorized data collection nodes within our test screens.

5. Record Rectification & Data Deletion

Aspirants retain full structural authority over their progress logs. Account clearing, deletion of score track histories, or profile resets can be initiated through our designated structural helpdesks.
